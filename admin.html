GradSnaps

Gallery manager

How to use
  1. Add, rename, reorder or delete shoots and photos below. New photos are shrunk automatically.
  2. Click Download updated gallery at the bottom. You get a ZIP file.
  3. Unzip it, copy gallery-data.js and the images folder into your GradSnaps site folder (say yes to replace / merge), then upload the site again.

Nothing on your live website changes until you do step 3, so you can’t break the site from here. The first photo of each shoot is its cover. New shoots appear first on the website.
